StatLab Zim — Interactive Statistical Analysis

Designed and shipped StatLab Zim, an open-source Streamlit app used to teach and apply statistics. Handles CSV uploads, data-quality profiling, 13-metric descriptive summaries, 6 interactive Plotly visualizations, and 5 hypothesis tests (Pearson, Spearman, linear regression, t-test, chi-square) with plain-language interpretations. Includes CSV/PNG exports, 19 pytest tests, and polished UX with spinners, empty-states, and help tooltips. Deployed on Streamlit Cloud.
